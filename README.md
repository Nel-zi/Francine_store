# Francine_store
 
Project overview

● Francine Stores, a laptop vendor on Aliexpress, aims to analyze product brand
trends on the platform to better understand market demands. They require
a data engineer to scrape, transform, and load Aliexpress data into their
database for advanced analysis.

Business Problem 

● The main challenge is extracting accurate and current data from Aliexpress,
which is dynamic and complex due to its large scale and frequent updates.

Objectives

● To develop a scalable and robust system that periodically extracts data from
Aliexpress, ensuring the data is clean, well-structured, and updated.

Benefits

1. Market Insight: Understand real-time market trends and consumer
preferences.

2. Strategic Decisions: Data-driven strategies for stock management and
marketing.

3. Competitive Advantage: Staying ahead by leveraging up-to-date data.
